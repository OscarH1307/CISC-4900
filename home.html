Introduction

The Pine Bluff Silent Film Festival has been held annually since its inception in 1994. It is thought by some to be the oldest silent film festival in the United States in which the silent films are shown with full orchestral accompaniment. From inauspicious beginnings, the festival has grown to have an international reputation and has been recognized at major film festivals in New York City; London, England and Portanome Italy as a world-class festival and the only one, which regularly presents silent films with, live orchestral accompaniment and guest stars.

Team

Kathy Majewska

Ms. Majewska's danced professionally with Wadim Sulima Ballet-charactor Company, All Nations COmpany, and with Matteo's company. She founded her own company the Cameron Highland Dancers which performed at the United Nations. She works with the Pine Bluff Symphony and has choreographed such works as "Peter and the Wolf' and El Amor Brujo. Working with John Beatty and Foster Hirsch in conection with the Pine Bluff Film Festival, has been the greatest priviledge of all!

John Beatty

John Beatty had a career in the performing arts, beginning as a puppeteer and later as an actor and singer appearing in a number of New York stage productions and commercially released films, often playing the roles of monsters. rof. Beatty is the director of the American Indian Institute at Brooklyn College as well as curator of the anthropology department's traveling museum. His publications include several books and numerous articles in scientific journals. He has been Master of Ceremonies and lecturer at the Festival since its inception in 1994.

Charles Jones Evans

Charles Jones Evans is currently Music Director of the Pine Bluff Symphony Orchestra, praised for his "exuberant", his style as "straight-ahead, no-nonsense" with "unflagging energy". A native of Dallas, he has held conducting positions with numerous professional orchestras and music festivals, including the Eastern Philharmonic (Greensboro, NC), the Memphis Symphony, the Mississippi Symphony and the Eastman Philharmonic (Rochester, NY).

Foster Hirsch

Foster Hirsch is professor of film at Brooklyn College and the author of 15 books including "The Dark Side of the Screen: Film Noir"; "Acting Hollywood Style"; "A Method to their Madness: The History of the Actors Studio". His newest book, "Kurt Weill on Stage: From Berlin to Broadway" will be published in the Fall of 2001 by Alfred Knopf. He is host for the American Film Institute tributes in New York and has been the interviewer for the Pine Bluff Film Festival since its inception.
